# Sonny's Story Quest

A 12-night bedtime reading adventure for a three-and-a-half-year-old, played on a phone in bed with a grown-up.

Each night takes about five minutes: meet one letter sound, trace it, hunt for it, pick the picture that starts with it, then read a short story starring Sonny, Meadow, Dada and Momma with dragons, dinosaurs and monsters. Every few nights the sounds get pushed together into real words.

## The plan

| Night | Focus | Story |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s | Super Sonny and the Sleepy Snake |
| 2 | a | The Alligator and the Apple |
| 3 | t | Tiny T-rex Cannot Sleep |
| 4 | blend: at, sat | The Dragon Who Would Not Sit |
| 5 | p | The Purple Monster's Pizza |
| 6 | i | Iggy the Itchy Iguana |
| 7 | n | The Noisy Nest |
| 8 | blend: sit, tap, pan, nap | Nap, Dragon, Nap |
| 9 | m | Meadow and the Mini Monster |
| 10 | d | Dada and the Dinosaur Dance |
| 11 | blend: dad, sad, mad, mat | Sad Dragon, Mad Dragon |
| 12 | read whole sentences | Sonny Reads to Meadow |

Why it is built this way:

- **Letter sounds before letter names**, in the s-a-t-p-i-n order used by systematic phonics programmes, because those six letters make real words fastest. m and d follow so he can read "dad".
- **Hearing first sounds** (the picture-picking game) comes before blending. It is the strongest early predictor of reading.
- **Multi-sensory**: see the letter, trace it, say it, hear it in a story.
- **Shared reading**: the grown-up reads, with a question to talk about on most pages. On blending nights the words he can sound out are gold and he reads those.
- **Bedtime-friendly**: dark, warm colours, no timers, no wrong-answer buzzers, and each night ends with three slow dragon breaths.

## Files

- `app.html` is the app (page body, styles and script in one file).
- `build.sh` wraps it into `index.html`, a standalone page you can open or host anywhere.

Stickers and optional family photos are saved in the phone's browser only. No photos are stored in this repository.
